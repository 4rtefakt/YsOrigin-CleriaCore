CleriaCore {version} for macOS (Apple Silicon)
=============================================

Welcome! CleriaCore plays your own copy of Ys Origin on a modern engine, natively on your Mac. It
contains none of the game's files: you need your own copy of Ys Origin from Steam.

Before you start: get the game files
------------------------------------
Steam for Mac does not install Ys Origin, because it is a Windows game. Any of these works:
- Copy the "Ys Origin" folder from a Windows PC's Steam library (steamapps/common/Ys Origin) to your Mac.
- Install it with Steam inside a CrossOver or Whisky bottle: CleriaCore finds it there by itself.

First run
---------
1. Drag CleriaCore.app into your Applications folder.
2. Open it. CleriaCore is not notarized by Apple yet, so the first time macOS blocks it. Open
   System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to CleriaCore.
   (On macOS 14 and older you can also right-click the app and choose Open.)
   From a terminal, this does the same:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/CleriaCore.app
3. CleriaCore lists the Ys Origin installs it found. Pick yours, or browse to the folder you copied. It
   unpacks the game data once, in about 20 seconds. Your copy of the game is only read, never changed.
4. You land on the title screen. Press F1 at any time for the settings and the mods.

Good to know
------------
- Your settings, saves and mods live in ~/Library/Application Support/CleriaCore.
- Mods: open Mods on the launcher (or F1 > Mods) and press "Get mods", or double-click a .cleriamod file.
- To update, replace CleriaCore.app with the new one. To uninstall, move it to the Trash.
- macOS support is the newest: please tell us how it runs on your Mac.

Something wrong? Open an issue and say what you expected to see:
