CleriaCore {version} for Windows
================================

Welcome! CleriaCore plays your own copy of Ys Origin on a modern engine. It contains none of the game's
files: you need Ys Origin installed from Steam.

First run
---------
1. Unzip this folder somewhere you can write to, such as Documents\CleriaCore or D:\Games\CleriaCore
   (not Program Files).
2. Double-click cleria-view.exe.
   Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because CleriaCore is not signed yet: click "More info",
   then "Run anyway".
3. CleriaCore lists the Ys Origin installs it found in your Steam libraries. Pick yours, or browse to the
   folder that holds yso_win.exe. It unpacks the game data once, in about 20 seconds. Your Ys Origin
   install is only read, never changed.
4. You land on the title screen. Press F1 at any time for the settings and the mods.

Good to know
------------
- Your settings, saves and mods live in Saved Games\CleriaCore. Your original Ys Origin saves are left alone.
- Mods: open Mods on the launcher (or F1 > Mods) and press "Get mods", or double-click a .cleriamod file.
- To update, unzip the new version over this folder (or into a new one). Your saves are not in it.
- To uninstall, delete this folder. Delete Saved Games\CleriaCore too if you want your saves gone.
- CleriaCore needs a graphics card with Direct3D 12 or Vulkan (any from the last ten years).
